The first version will not be perfect. Play it, then change one thing at a
time.

## Next asks to try

> The dragon should flap its wings and bob a little while it flies.

> When you collect 5 gems in a row, play a happy sound and show a little burst
> of sparkles.

> Add thunderclouds to dodge, and a level 2 where they move faster.

## Tips for working with Claude

- **One thing at a time.** "Make the dragon slower" works better than a long
  list of wishes.
- **Play after every change**, and say what you see. For example: "the
  dragon is too fast" or "I can't see the gems on a phone."
- **Say how it should feel**, not how to code it. For example: "Make it
  bouncier."
- **Ask for tests** when something must keep working: "Write a test that
  touching a thundercloud ends the round."
- **Keep the old version** if you are unsure. Ask: "Try a version where the
  camera follows the dragon, on a new branch, and don't merge it yet."

## Adding pictures and sounds

Claude can draw shapes, characters and backgrounds in code. These load fast.
If the child draws something on paper, photograph it and give it to Claude:
"Make the dragon look like this drawing." Every picture, sound and font must
be inside the game. Nothing is loaded from other websites.
