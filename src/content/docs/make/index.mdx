You will make a game at your own web address. Your first game will be basic:
a few shapes and one thing to do. That way you see results quickly, and each
change makes the game better. Then friends play it with you from their own
homes.

**Who does what:** The child has the ideas, answers Claude's questions, and
plays. The grown-up handles the accounts, types, and presses the buttons. An
AI helper named Claude writes the code.

**Time:** about an hour the first time. After that, as long as you like.

## What you need

- **A computer** with a web browser. A tablet or phone works too, but a
  computer is easiest the first time.
- **A GitHub account** (free) at [github.com](https://github.com/signup).
  GitHub keeps your game's code and puts it on the web. You must be 13 or
  older to have an account. For a younger child, it is a grown-up's account.
- **Claude, Pro plan or higher**, at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). It
  includes Claude Code, the AI helper this guide uses. The account holder
  must be 18 or older. Stay with your child while they use it.

:::note[Other AI helpers]
Without a Claude plan, you can build from the starter template with Codex in
ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot Free. Both read the same rules. Their buttons
differ a little from this guide.

Codex in ChatGPT is in the free plan for now. You must be at least 13 to use
it. Anyone under 18 needs a parent's permission. GitHub Copilot Free is for
people aged 13 or older.
:::

## How a game gets built

Claude does not change your game straight away. Each change takes the same
short path, and you decide when it goes in.

<ArcadeDiagram which="build" />

Four new words, in a few words each:

- **Repository:** your game's home on GitHub. It holds the game's code.
- **Branch:** a copy of your game for trying a change.
- **Pull request:** a request to add the change to your game.
- **Merge:** adding the change to the main game. It is one button on GitHub.

**A change only goes live after the merge.** Until then, your game on the web
stays as it was. After the merge, GitHub publishes the new version in a
minute or two.

## Steps to your first game

1. [Start from the template](/make/copy/): your game's own repository, in
   five minutes.
2. [Connect Claude to your game](/make/ai-helper/): once, on the web.
3. [Make your first game](/make/first-game/): one idea, a few questions, a
   basic game.
4. [Put it on the web](/make/publish/): one setting, then play it at your own
   address.
5. [Improve your game](/make/your-game/): two or three changes at a time.
6. [Add your game to the arcade](/make/arcade/): a tile on each of the
   family's devices.
7. [Play your game with friends](/make/together/): up to four devices, in
   other homes too.

If something goes wrong: [Fix common problems](/make/stuck/).
