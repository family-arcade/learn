You will make a game at your own web address. Your first game is basic: a few shapes and one thing to do. You see results quickly, and each change makes it better.

**Who does what:** The child has the ideas and plays. The grown-up handles the accounts and presses the buttons. Claude writes the code.

**Time:** about an hour the first time.

## What you need

- **A computer** with a web browser. A tablet works too, but a computer is easiest.
- **A GitHub account** (free) at [github.com](https://github.com/signup).
  GitHub keeps your game's code and puts it on the web. You must be 13 or older to have an account. For a younger child, it is a grown-up's account.
- **Claude, Pro plan or higher**, at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). It includes Claude Code, the AI helper this guide uses. The account holder must be 18 or older. Stay with your child while they use it.

:::note[Other AI helpers]
Without a Claude plan, you can use Codex in ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot Free. Both read the same rules. Their buttons differ a little from this guide.

Codex in ChatGPT is in the free plan for now. You must be at least 13 to use
it. Anyone under 18 needs a parent's permission. GitHub Copilot Free is for
people aged 13 or older.
:::

## How a game gets built

Claude does not change your game straight away. You decide when each change goes in.

<ArcadeDiagram which="build" />

Four new words:

- **Repository:** your game's home on GitHub. It holds the game's code.
- **Branch:** a copy of your game for trying a change.
- **Pull request:** a request to add the change to your game.
- **Merge:** adding the change to your game. It is one button on GitHub.

**A change only goes live after the merge.** Until then, your game on the web stays as it was. After the merge, GitHub publishes the new version in a minute or two.

1. [Start from the template](/make/copy/): your game's home, in five minutes.
2. [Connect Claude to your game](/make/ai-helper/): once, on the web.

4. [Put it on the web](/make/publish/): one setting, then play it.
5. [Improve your game](/make/your-game/): two or three changes at a time.
6. [Add your game to the arcade](/make/arcade/): a tile on each family device.
7. [Play your game with friends](/make/together/): up to four devices, in other homes too.
