GitHub Pages is GitHub's free web hosting. You turn it on once. After that,
every merge goes on the web by itself.

## Turn on GitHub Pages

1. In your repository, click the <GhIcon name="gear" />**Settings** tab at
   the top. Its icon is a cog.
2. In the list on the left, click <GhIcon name="browser" />**Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**.

## Publish the game

A workflow is the set of steps GitHub follows to test and publish your game.
Run it once by hand:

1. Click the <GhIcon name="play" />**Actions** tab at the top.
2. On the left, click **Deploy to GitHub Pages**.
3. On the right, click **Run workflow**. A small box opens.
4. In that box, click the green **Run workflow** button.
5. Wait a minute or two, until the dot next to the new run turns green.

:::note[Earlier runs]
The list may also show older runs, from before Pages was on. Each has a
green tick. Open one and it says "Not published yet". That is expected.
:::

## Play your game

Your game is now at:

```
https://<your-github-name>.github.io/<game-name>/
```

Open that address and play your first game. It is on the web, and it is yours.

:::tip
Bookmark your game's address. On a phone or tablet, add it to the home
screen.
:::

## Every change from now on

Every change follows the same loop:

**Ask → merge → play.**

Claude makes the change on a branch. You merge it. A minute or two later, it
is live. Reload your game's page to see it.
