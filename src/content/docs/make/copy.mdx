## Create your repository

A repository is your game's home on GitHub. It holds the game's code and
files.

1. Sign in to GitHub.
2. Open the
   **[game starter](https://github.com/family-arcade/arcade-game-starter)**.
3. Make your own copy of it:
   1. Click the green **Use this template**
      button.
   2. Click **Create a new repository**.
4. Give your game a name, in small letters with dashes instead of spaces:
   `dragon-dash`. You can change it later.
5. Choose who can see the code:
   1. Choose **Public**.
   2. Click **Create repository**.

:::note[Why public]
GitHub puts your game on the web for free only from a **public**
repository. Public means anyone can read the game's code. Your family's
players and saves are not in it: they stay on each device.

- **To keep the code private,** GitHub's paid Pro plan can publish from a
  private repository. The game's page is still public: anyone with the link
  can play it.
- **Private games for a family** may come to the Family Arcade one day,
  possibly as a paid option. They are not available yet.
:::

## Put it on the web

1. In your new repository, click the <GhIcon name="gear" />**Settings** tab
   at the top. Its icon is a cog.
2. In the list on the left, click <GhIcon name="browser" />**Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**.
4. Publish the game for the first time:
   1. Click the <GhIcon name="play" />**Actions** tab at the top.
   2. On the left, click **Deploy to GitHub Pages**.
   3. On the right, click **Run workflow**. A small box opens.
   4. In that box, click the green **Run workflow** button.
5. Wait a minute or two. When the dot next to the run turns green, your
   game is on the web.

Your game is now at:

```
https://<your-github-name>.github.io/<game-name>/
```

Open that address. You see your game's empty home page. It shows the game's
name, who is playing and a **Play together** button. It is empty because you
have not asked for a game yet. To check that it works, try **Play together**
on two devices.

:::tip
Bookmark your game's address. On a phone or tablet, add it to the home
screen.
:::
