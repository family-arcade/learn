Claude Code will help you build your game. You can also use ChatGPT's Codex.
We have generally had more success making games with Claude.

On the web, Claude Code needs nothing installed. It works on a copy of your
game in the cloud. Your game changes only when you merge.

## Connect Claude once

1. Open **[claude.ai/code](https://claude.ai/code)**.
2. Sign in to Claude with a grown-up's account.
3. Connect GitHub:
   1. Click **Sign in with GitHub**.
   2. Allow the connection.
4. When you are asked, install the **Claude** GitHub app.
5. Choose your game's repository. Choosing only that repository is fine.
6. Go back to Claude and pick your repository.

You can also use the Claude app on an iPad or phone. Open the **Code** tab.
You can follow a session started on the computer and merge its change from
the tablet.

## Questions for your child

Before it builds the first game, Claude asks a few short questions, one at a
time. Who are you in the game? What do you collect or avoid? How do you win?
Your child answers. If they are stuck, Claude offers two or three choices.

Sit your child at the keyboard, or let them say the answers for you to type.
They can also [talk instead of typing](/make/first-game/#let-your-child-talk).

## Rules Claude already knows

The starter template contains a file called `AGENTS.md`, the rules for AI
helpers. Claude reads it before changing anything. Codex reads it too.
Nothing needs installing. From it, Claude already knows:

- to ask your child a few questions first, and to keep the first game small;
- how the game is put together and how to test it;
- to check the game before it says a change is done: the tests, the build,
  and nothing loaded from other websites;
- to keep play together working;
- what every game here keeps: no ads, no trackers, nothing from other
  websites, no chat, playable by touch and keyboard.

You do not need to repeat any of that. Talk about the game.
