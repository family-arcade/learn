You are going to make a game that lives at your own web address and that
anyone you send it to can play, together, from different homes.

**Who does what:** the child has the ideas, says what to change, and plays.
The grown-up handles the accounts, types, and presses the buttons. An AI
helper, Claude, writes the code.

**Time:** about an hour the first time. After that, as long as you like.

## What you need

- **A computer** with a web browser. A tablet or phone works too, but a
  computer is easiest the first time.
- **A GitHub account** (free) at [github.com](https://github.com/signup).
  GitHub keeps your game's code and puts it on the web. You must be 13 or
  older to have one, so for younger children it is the grown-up's.
- **Claude, Pro plan or higher**, at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). It
  includes Claude Code, the AI helper this guide uses. The account holder
  must be 18 or older.

:::note[No Claude plan?]
Codex in ChatGPT (in the free plan for now; 13+, with a parent's permission
under 18) and GitHub Copilot Free (13+) can build from the starter too: they
read the same rules. The buttons differ a little from this guide.
:::

## The steps

1. [Your own copy](/make/copy/): copy the starter and put it on the web.
2. [Meet your AI helper](/make/ai-helper/): connect Claude to your game.
3. [Your first change](/make/first-change/): ask, merge, play.
4. [Make it your own](/make/your-game/): your idea, one step at a time.
5. [Play together](/make/together/): with family and friends, anywhere.
6. [Put it in the arcade](/make/arcade/): your tile on the family's devices.

If something goes wrong: [When something breaks](/make/stuck/).
