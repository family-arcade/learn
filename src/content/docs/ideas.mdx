## Small changes to try

<Prompt>Make the background a sunset instead of night.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Make the gems sparkle a little.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Add a timer, and when 10 seconds are left make it flash and tick.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Give each player a hat in their own colour.</Prompt>

## Games to start from

<Prompt>Make a new game: you are a hungry frog on a lily pad. Flies buzz past and you catch them with your tongue by tapping. Catch 20 to win.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Make a new game: two to four players race paper boats down a river, steering around rocks. The first to the waterfall wins.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Make a new game: you are a unicorn bouncing on clouds. Each cloud you land on turns into a rainbow. Don't fall off the bottom.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Make a new game: a maze where you are a little robot looking for three keys before the battery runs out.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Make a new game: everyone builds a tower from falling blocks at the same time. The tallest tower when the music stops wins.</Prompt>

## Making it feel better

<Prompt>Make the jump feel bouncier.</Prompt>

<Prompt>It's too hard at the start. Make the first 30 seconds easy, then get harder.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Add a little squish when the frog lands.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Make the buttons bigger so they are easy to tap on a phone.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Add a calm version for when the device is set to reduce motion: no shaking or flashing.</Prompt>

## Sounds and pictures

<Prompt>Make a happy "pling" in code when you catch something. Add a mute button.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Here is a photo of my drawing of the dragon. Make the dragon in the game look like this, with the same colours.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Draw the background in code: hills, a few trees and a big moon.</Prompt>

## Asking Claude for help

<Prompt>Explain in simple words how the game decides who wins.</Prompt>

<Prompt>Something feels wrong: the frog sometimes catches a fly it didn't touch. Please find out why and fix it, and write a test for it.</Prompt>

<Prompt>What would be a fun thing to add next? Give me three small ideas.</Prompt>
