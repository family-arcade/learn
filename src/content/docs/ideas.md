Copy any prompt into Claude. Change the words to make it yours.

## First changes to Star Catch

> Make the background a sunset instead of night.

> The stars should wobble a little as they fall.

> When the timer has 10 seconds left, make it flash and tick.

> Give each player a hat in their own colour.

## Games to start from

> Make a new game: you are a hungry frog on a lily pad. Flies buzz past and
> you catch them with your tongue by tapping. Catch 20 to win.

> Make a new game: two to four players race paper boats down a river,
> steering around rocks. The first to the waterfall wins.

> Make a new game: you are a unicorn bouncing on clouds. Each cloud you land
> on turns into a rainbow. Don't fall off the bottom.

> Make a new game: a maze where you are a little robot looking for three
> keys before the battery runs out.

> Make a new game: everyone builds a tower from falling blocks at the same
> time. The tallest tower when the music stops wins.

Add "Keep play together working" to any prompt so friends can join.

## Making it feel better

> Make the jump feel bouncier.

> It's too hard at the start. Make the first 30 seconds easy, then get
> harder.

> Add a little squish when the frog lands.

> Make the buttons bigger so they are easy to tap on a phone.

> Add a calm version for when the device is set to reduce motion: no
> shaking or flashing.

## Sounds and pictures

> Make a happy "pling" in code when you catch something. Add a mute button.

> Here is a photo of my drawing of the dragon. Make the dragon in the game
> look like this, with the same colours.

> Draw the background in code: hills, a few trees and a big moon.

## Asking for help

> Explain in simple words how the game decides who wins.

> Something feels wrong: the frog sometimes catches a fly it didn't touch.
> Please find out why and fix it, and write a test for it.

> What would be a fun thing to add next? Give me three small ideas.
