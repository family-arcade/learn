The Family Arcade started on a family holiday: a dad, his daughters and his
son making browser games together with AI helpers, then playing them on the
family's iPads. The games grew (unicorns, ships, a city-eating blob), and so
did the wish to play them with cousins in other homes.

This guide and the game starter are how other families can do the same: make
a game with their children, and play each other's.

It is a family project, not a business. No ads, no tracking, no accounts to
sell.
