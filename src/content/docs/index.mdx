## A basic game in an afternoon

The first game is basic: a few shapes and one thing to do. It works quickly, so your child sees results.

<Steps>

1. **Start from the template.** It becomes your game's home on GitHub.

2. **Tell Claude your idea in one sentence.** Claude asks a few questions, then builds a basic game.

   <Prompt>Make a game where you are a dragon collecting gems in the clouds.</Prompt>

3. **Put it on the web.** One setting gives your game its own address.

4. **Make it better,** two or three changes at a time. Play after each one.

5. **Play with friends** in the Family Arcade.

</Steps>

## Playing together in the Family Arcade

Friends add your game to their own Family Arcade at [familyarcade.eu](https://familyarcade.eu). One device shows four letters. The others type them.

## Three things to start

- **An hour,** and a computer.
- **A free GitHub account.** It keeps your game's code and puts it on the web. For a child under 13, it is a grown-up's account.
- **An AI helper.** This guide uses Claude Code, part of a paid Claude plan held by a grown-up. [Free options for teenagers](/make/) exist too.

## Safe by design

- **No ads and no tracking,** in the arcade and in every game made from the starter template.
- **Only people with your code** can play with your child. There is no chat and no public list of players.
- **Players and saves stay on your device.** There are no accounts to sign up for.
