## A first game in an afternoon

<Steps>

1. **Start from the template.** Your game gets its own web address in about
   ten minutes. [How](/make/copy/)

2. **Ask for your game in one sentence.** For example: "Make a game where you
   are a dragon collecting gems in the clouds." [How](/make/first-change/)

3. **Improve it one small step at a time.** Play after every change and say
   what to change next. [How](/make/your-game/)

4. **Play it with family and friends,** and add it to the
   [Family Arcade](https://familyarcade.eu) on every device.
   [How](/make/together/)

</Steps>

## Getting started needs three things

- **An hour,** and a computer for the first time.
- **A free GitHub account.** It keeps your game's code and puts it on the
  web. It is the grown-up's account for children under 13.
- **An AI helper.** This guide uses Claude Code, part of a paid Claude plan
  held by a grown-up. [Free options for teenagers](/make/) are listed too.

## Safe by design

- **No ads and no tracking,** in the arcade and in every game made from the
  starter.
- **Only people with your code** can play with your child. There is no chat
  and no public list of players.
- **Players and saves stay on your device.** There are no accounts to sign up
  for.
