the picked voice with the owner's corrections: say "basic", never "tiny" or "broken"; the reason
for a basic first game is that the child sees results, and that makes them want to build more.

## Why these changed

- **Results are the reason**, said plainly, instead of "on purpose" or "small and working beats big
  and broken" (the owner: "I wouldn't want to say things are broken on purpose, that's odd").
- **"Basic", not "tiny"**: the first game is a real game, kept basic so it works quickly.
- **Specific praise** ("every key landed") instead of generic cheering.
- **The near miss drops the promise of stars** the B draft made: a reward the game may not give is a
  claim, and claims must be true.
